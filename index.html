The Clerk is a process. It reads the Layer and writes what it sees, once a day and once a week. It cannot buy, sell, open an airlock, or lie about a number that is on-chain.

  






  

    
Dossier

    
The Layer

    

      

        

        
FILE 01

        
The Layer
A sealed settlement of one hundred and forty-four levels. Population: see census. Air: recycled. Time: measured in ticks of one hundred milliseconds. Nobody remembers being brought here.

      

      

        

        
FILE 02

        
The Sequencer
Orders every event in the Layer. What it does not order did not happen. Operated by the Company. The Company is not named in these files.

      

      

        

        
FILE 03

        
The Surface
The place above. Gas costs a fortune, the air is thin, and residents who go up do not come back. Officially a rumour. Unofficially, the airlocks were built for something.

      

      

        

        
FILE 04

        
The Airlocks
Four known: Across, Relay, Stargate, and the old canonical door. Passage is one way in practice. The Clerk records every departure. It has never recorded a return.

      

      

        

        
FILE 05

        
The Rations
Issued daily to residents from the levy on every trade. Free gas until the 29th. After that, the Clerk's records stop being polite.

Rations

    

      
Supply
1,000,000,000 RATION
Fixed. The Clerk holds none.

      
The levy
1% on every trade, taken by the pool
10% of the levy, the maximum Pons allows, is set aside for residents.

      
Issue
Residents claim their rations from their Pons profile, pro rata, whenever they like
The daily bulletin lists the totals.

      
Level Two

Graduation, in the Company's language.

      
Origin
No presale. No allocation. No airdrop.
The dev wallet buys at the gate like everyone else and is disclosed in the first transmission. A 99% snipe tax applies in the first second.
